Privacy & Biometric Data Policy

Last updated: September 2026 • Compliant with DPDP Act 2023 & GDPR

1. Purpose Limitation

StyleMirror processes portrait photographs strictly and solely for generating hair and beard style previews at the user's explicit request. We never sell, rent, or transfer biometric or photographic information to third-party data brokers or marketing firms.

2. 24-Hour Automatic Deletion & Instant Purge

All uploaded original selfies and AI-generated outputs are stored on encrypted, isolated local storage and are automatically permanently wiped within 24 hours of creation. Users can click the "Delete My Photo" button at any time to immediately destroy all photos and database records.

3. No AI Model Training

Your photographs are never used to train, fine-tune, or calibrate artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Upstream AI providers process image transformations in memory under strict Zero-Data-Retention privacy agreements.

4. Security & Access Control

Storage files are non-public and protected by cryptographic HMAC tokens with time-limited expirations. All communications between your browser and our servers use HTTPS/TLS 1.3 encryption.

5. Contact & Grievance Redressal

For inquiries, rights requests under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or immediate manual deletion requests, contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@stylemirror.ai.
